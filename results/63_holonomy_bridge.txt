Discrete from continuous: pointwise VQ vs holonomy (integrate)

  pointwise VQ (quantise each step, compose):  acc = 0.132
  holonomy (integrate the path, quantise once): acc = 1.000

  holonomy additivity h(path1+path2) == h(path1)+h(path2): 50.5%

=> the discrete emerges from the continuous only through the HOLONOMY (the integral);
   it is path-dependent (the paradox), and that is exactly what makes the bridge
   non-trivial -- a trivial pointwise map cannot carry the algebra.
