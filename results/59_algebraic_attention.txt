Algebraic attention block: softmax (O(T^2)) vs linear (O(T), associative)

     T  softmax ms  linear ms   ratio
    64         0.4        2.2     0.2x
   256         1.6        9.7     0.2x
  1024        25.6       38.7     0.7x
  2048        99.1       77.4     1.3x

relative-position task (train T=20): accuracy of the two blocks
   softmax: T=20 1.000  T=40 1.000  T=80 1.000
    linear: T=20 0.990  T=40 0.072  T=80 0.100

=> linear attention is asymptotically cheaper (O(T)) and here keeps the task;
   the associativity (monoid) reassociation is the algebraic lever.
